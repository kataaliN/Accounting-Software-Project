Help — Financial Reports

  • Date Range — Filter ledger entries by date. Leave blank to use all approved entries ever posted.
  • Generate — Pulls live data from approved journal entries and account balances.
  • Data source — Reports read from both the opening account balance and posted ledger entries. If no entries exist in a date range, the opening balance is used.
  • Yellow notice — Shown when no ledger entries exist yet; balances come from opening account balances only.
  • Trial Balance — All accounts with their total debits and credits. Debits must equal credits.
  • Income Statement — Revenue − Expenses = Net Income / Loss.
  • Balance Sheet — Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
  • Retained Earnings — Opening RE + Net Income − Dividends = Ending RE.
  • Save — Saves a snapshot to localStorage with a timestamp.
  • Print — Opens the browser print dialog showing only the report.
  • Email — Delivers the summary to the Admin or Manager in-app inbox.
