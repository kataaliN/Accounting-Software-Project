    Help — Journal Transactions

    • Debits first — Debit lines appear above credit lines.
    • Multiple lines — Use "+ Add Debit / Credit" for compound entries.
    • Must balance — Total debits must equal total credits.
    • Submit Entry — Saves the entry as Pending. You must then click Review on it in the table to approve or reject it before it posts to the ledger.
    • Reset — Clears the form without submitting.
    • Pending banner — Shows how many accountant entries need review.
    • Review button — Opens approve/reject modal. Rejection requires a comment.
    • PR link — Click any PR to view full entry details.
