Help & Documentation

Your workspace for recording and managing financial transactions.

  • Chart of Accounts — View all financial accounts in read-only mode.
  • Journal Entries — Create and submit balanced journal transactions for manager review.
  • Reports — View financial summaries and generated statements.
  • Search & Filter — Narrow accounts by name, number, category, normal side, or status.
  • Click an account name — Goes to the full ledger page for that account.
  • Read-only — Editing is restricted to Administrators only.
  • Create entries — Select accounts from the chart of accounts, add debit and credit lines, and submit.
  • Must balance — Total debits must equal total credits before you can submit.
  • Multiple lines — Add multiple debit or credit lines for compound entries.
  • Reset — Clears the form. Once submitted, entries cannot be deleted.
  • View status — See all your submitted entries and their Pending / Approved / Rejected status.

View income statements and balance sheets generated from posted journal entries.
