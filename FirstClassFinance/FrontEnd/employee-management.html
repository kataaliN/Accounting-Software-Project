Help & Documentation

Manage all system users from this page.

  • Current Employees table — Lists all employees with their name, email, date of birth, and current status.
  • Activate / Deactivate — Toggle an employee's access on or off without deleting their record.
  • Manually Invite Employee — Submit a new employee's details. Their request will appear on the Access Requests page for admin approval before becoming active.

The Administrator Dashboard is your central hub for navigating all management areas of the system.

Create and manage the chart of financial accounts.

  • Filter bar — Search and filter accounts by category, normal side, or status.
  • Click a row — Opens the account ledger popup.
  • Edit Account — Load an account into the form for editing.
  • Create Account — Fill in the form and submit to add a new account.
  • Approve — Grants Active status and moves the employee to Employee Management.
  • Reject — Permanently removes the request.
  • Waffle menu (⠿) — Opens the navigation sidebar. Current page is highlighted in green.
  • Profile menu — Click your name or avatar for account options including Logout.
  • Help button (?) — Opens this help panel on any page.
